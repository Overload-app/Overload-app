This Privacy Policy explains what information Overload ("we," "us") collects, how we use it, and who we share it with. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

WhatWhy
Name and email addressTo create and manage your account

2. Who We Share Data With

We use a small number of third-party service providers to run the App. We don't sell your data to anyone.

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles account authentication. Your account and app data are stored here.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — processes your quiz answers, coach chat messages, and any meal photos you submit, in order to generate your program, nutrition targets, and coach responses. Message content is sent to Anthropic's API for this purpose.
  • Stripe — processes payments and manages subscriptions. We never receive or store your full card number.
  • Resend — sends account-related emails (confirmation, password reset).
  • Vercel — hosts the App itself.

3. Health & Fitness Data

Some information you provide (injuries, body measurements, goals) may be considered sensitive health-related information in certain jurisdictions. We collect this only to generate your personalized program and do not sell it or use it for advertising purposes.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data is removed from our active systems, though some information may be retained where required for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention purposes (e.g. payment records).

5. Your Choices

You can review and update your profile information within the App. You can request account deletion by contacting us. You can unsubscribe from any marketing email (if we ever send one) at any time.

6. Children

Overload is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under that age.

7. Security

We use industry-standard practices (encrypted connections, hashed passwords, access-controlled databases) to protect your data, but no system is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do.
